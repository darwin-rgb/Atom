About hydrogen

  • The lightest element, with an atomic mass of about 1.008 u.
  • Its common form has no neutrons, just one proton and one electron.
  • A colourless, odourless gas (H₂) at room temperature.
  • The most abundant element in the universe, about three quarters of ordinary matter by mass.
  • Placed in group 1, yet a nonmetal.
Distances are in Bohr radii (a₀), about 0.053 nm each.
